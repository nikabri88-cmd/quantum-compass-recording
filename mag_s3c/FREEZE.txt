MAG-S3c freeze before the confirmatory run, 10 November 2026 (UTC)

The protocol v0.4, the code, the requirements, the structure file, the reference calibration and the Colab notebook
were frozen at 19:40 UTC, after the calibration (three rounds), two blind code reviews, the check of the
environment and the blind pipeline check in Colab (TPU v6e-1, notebook build f994d9b191aa9dbb). Their SHA-256 below are
posted in Telegram before the confirmatory run. Cell 5 of the notebook starts the run only after the answer
«запускай» is typed into it; it checks every file against the SHA-256 embedded in the notebook and the marker of the
passed blind check of the same build, and mag_s3c.py and pipeline.sh compute nothing at the rates of the hypotheses
without that confirmation.
Paths are relative to this folder. mag_s3b.py, mag_s3.py, analyze.py, mag_s2.py and physics.json are the frozen
files of MAG-S3b v0.2.0 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24079875), which carry MAG-S3 v0.1.0 and MAG-S2 v0.1.2; code/data/6PU0.pdb
is the crystal structure of pigeon cryptochrome 4 (ClCry4a) from the Protein Data Bank.

Checks in Colab, 10 November (TPU v6e-1, 44 processes). On build 895803767c9c0b57 cell 3 ran the 29 fast tests and
repeated the calibration C1–C6; against the reference calibration of round 3 it found 192 identical values, 31 equal
within the tolerances (residuals and errors of the two-site composite solver, 2·10⁻¹⁶ to 5·10⁻¹⁴) and none different.
Its blind pipeline check passed every step and ten invariants, and I11 failed, so the check ended with exit code 1,
deleted its output unread and left cell 5 closed; a first attempt from 15:22 UTC had stopped after the pair step when
the Colab tab lost its connection. I11 had divided rounding by the odd part of the crystal average, which the 222
average cancels almost entirely (results/checks/i11_first_blind_check.md); it now divides by the odd part of the
molecule, with the threshold unchanged. On the corrected build f994d9b191aa9dbb cell 3 ran the 30 fast tests with the
same comparison of the environment, and the blind check passed every step with exit code 0 in 48.1 minutes
(18:43:46–19:31:51 UTC): all eleven invariants held, I11 stood at 2.5·10⁻¹⁵ of the odd part of the molecule (the
normalization of the first check gave 5.2·10⁻¹² and would have failed again), and the output was deleted unread.
The logs of both builds are in results/checks/.

Before the freeze, the first smoke run of 10 November printed the verdicts H1–H5 of its substitute rates and its grid
of 60 directions in front of the author of the code. The protocol records this as a deviation; the line is sealed in
a file on Google Drive (SHA-256 3ea921e030a4df4eca4789a671f401a6cd8132814897ae978dfd040290a94d78), to be opened with the results of the
confirmatory run.
