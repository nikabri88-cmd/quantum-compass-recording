Clarified before release

- H1: the departure from linearity sits in the dipole components across the receptor axis and in the even range of
  the molecule (at chi_b = 0.1 pi, m = 0.1: |d_odd| 19.7% below the linear law, molecular even range 6.6% above its
  value at m = 1). The projection d_odd.z (+6.2%) and the receptor's even range (+5.6%) move together, so F stays
  linear within 1.9%. The draft had attributed the departure to higher odd harmonics, which carry only 1.5–2.7% of
  the molecule's odd power in these cells.
- Tilted CISS axis: the admissible region uses the eps_eff of every tilted cell directly. The receptor signal grows
  with tilt by 1.34–2.33 at m = 1, more than the projection G (1.18–1.60), because the receptor's even range shrinks
  with tilt; the single-slope bound through G is therefore conservative for tilted axes.
- H5: the undercoverage is specific to R2, the reference design at delta = 40%; R3 runs at the same delta and its
  exact interval includes 95%.
- Explanations of a null result: besides reciprocal CISS and apolar order with a free azimuth, non-reciprocal CISS of
  the polarisation forms also survives, because the recording does not limit those forms.
- E3: 6–10 is the ratio of median two-sided widths at eps_eff = 0; the protocol computes no separate E3 upper bound.
- The output metadata records the SHA-256 of 8 of the 16 published files; the notebook checks the remaining code
  files against the SHA-256 embedded in it when it starts.
- Of the 72 combinations with a rotated recombination axis (m = 0, alpha = 30 and 90 degrees) at p = 1, R1 admits 4.
- E6: the amplitude transfer alone admits 1286 of 2700 combinations in R1, both transfers together 1275.
- I5: the odd part at p = 0 is exactly zero; the half-value rule at p = 0.5 holds to 1e-16.
- E4: the statement on apolar stacks covers the two azimuth modes the protocol computed, free and fixed.
