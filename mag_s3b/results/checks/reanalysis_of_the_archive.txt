MAG-S3b – reanalysis of the confirmatory run outside Colab, 9 November 2026

The frozen analyze_s3b.py (SHA-256 a77198a4...) was run again in the calibration environment (Python 3.13.18,
NumPy 2.5.3, SciPy 1.18.1, RadicalPy 1.0.9, OpenBLAS SkylakeX, one BLAS thread) on the pair grid, the records
manifest, the estimates and the truth unpacked from results/confirmatory_run/confirmatory_s3b_light.tar.gz, with
the reference calibration results/calibration/round3:

  python3 -I analyze_s3b.py --pair RUN/pair --calibration round3 --records RUN/records \
      --estimates RUN/estimates --truth RUN/truth --out RUN/reanalysis

Every verdict and every number of results.json came out the same, with two kinds of exceptions.

- 27 values differ in the last bit: maps.E2.R1/R2/R3.translation.K_*.bound_dchi_p_cosb_by_S.S0.8, at most
  6.2e-16 relative.
- The fields metadata.time_utc, invariants.I7.truth_opened_at and metadata.blas[*].num_threads differ, as they
  record when and how the analysis ran.

The analysis step in Colab ran with 44 BLAS threads, because analyze_s3b.py, unlike mag_s3b.py and estimate.py, does
not pin the number of threads; the reanalysis ran with one thread. Every other step of the run (physics, receptor,
e3geo, generate, estimate, e4) recorded one BLAS thread, as the protocol requires. The 27 last-bit differences are
consistent with a different order of summation in multithreaded BLAS; no verdict depends on them.
